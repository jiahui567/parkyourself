Privacy policy

Park Yourself, an Android app by L. Last updated 28 October 2026.

Park Yourself remembers where you parked and what it costs. It keeps your data on your phone. An account is optional; this page says what leaves your phone, when, and how to delete it.

Without an account

Your parking data stays on your phone. Only these things reach the internet:

  • The car park list. At most once a day, when you open the app, it downloads the public list of car parks and their rates, and the rate reports waiting for review, from our server (Supabase). It sends no personal data; the server sees your phone's IP address, as any website does.
  • Reading the pillar sign. The app reads the photo of the sign on your phone with Google ML Kit. The photo does not leave your phone, but ML Kit sends Google identifiers for this installation and usage and diagnostic data.
  • Naming a new place. If you turn on Use location when parking and no saved or listed car park is nearby, Android's geocoder looks up a name for where you are, which sends your location to Google.
  • What you choose to open or share. Open in Google Maps opens Google Maps with your car's location. Share sends the parking details, or a photo, to the app you pick. Privacy policy in Settings opens this page in your browser.

With an account

You sign in with your email address, which identifies your account. Supabase emails your sign-in codes to it, and records each sign-in (its time, your IP address and the type of device or browser) to keep accounts secure.

While you are signed in, the app backs up everything you enter in it, except photos, and brings it to your other phones:

  • your cars: name, colour, and whether a car is the default or archived
  • your places: name, note, location if one was saved, and when you last parked there
  • the rates you saved for each place
  • your public holidays: dates and names
  • your parking: the place, level, bay, zone colour, how you left and the first shop, your note, the start and end times, the timer, the rates in force, the fee and the amount paid, and where the car was (with how accurate the reading was), if the location was on

Photos, the text the app reads from them and your settings are never uploaded.

The backup runs after you sign in, when the app opens or comes back to the front, a few seconds after you save something, when you tap Sync now, and before you sign out. It never runs in the background.

Rate reports

Signed in, you can report new rates for a car park on the list, or suggest one that is missing. A report holds the car park (or the name and location you give a new one), the rates, and an optional note and photo of the rate board. Anyone using the app can see the reported rates for a listed car park and when they were sent, but not who sent them. Only you and the developer can see who sent a report, its note and its photo, and a suggested new car park. If the rates are approved, they become part of the public car park list.

Where it is stored

On your phone, in the app's own storage. Your account, backup and reports are stored with Supabase, our server provider, currently in Japan. Each account can read and change only its own data, and report photos are in a private store that only you and the developer can open.

These web pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, and the account deletion page loads its code from jsDelivr. Like any website, both see your IP address when you visit.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. The one exception is ML Kit's usage data, above.
  • No tracking of your location. It is read only while the Park screen is open, and only if you allow it.
  • We do not sell your data, or share it with anyone other than the services named on this page.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: to take or pick photos of the pillar sign and your spot, and of a rate board for a report.
  • Location (while the app is open): to fill in the place and save where the car is.
  • Notifications: for the parked-car notification and timer reminders.

How long we keep it

Your account, backup and reports are kept until you delete your account. Something you delete in the app is marked as deleted in your backup, so your other phones delete it too; it is removed for good when you delete your account. Supabase's own security logs of sign-ins, which include your email address, may be kept for a time after that.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Back up and sync → Account → ⋮ → Delete account.
  • Without the app: delete your account on the web, or email from the address you signed in with.

Deleting your account deletes your account, backup, reports and their photos from our server. Rates of yours that were approved stay in the public car park list, with nothing to say who sent them. The data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
